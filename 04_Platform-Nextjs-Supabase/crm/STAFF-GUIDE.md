# AGS Services Master – staff guide

AGS is an independent private service, not a government service. Tell customers this if they ask.

## Every day
1. **Sign in** with your email and password. Your role decides what you can change.
2. **Dashboard**: look at the **Work queue** first (refunds to give, late applications, money due, ready to collect, journeys / darshans in the next 2 days). Click a line to open the list.

## A customer comes in
1. **Bookings & jobs › + New booking**.
2. Customer: type their **customer number (C00012) or mobile**. New person? Type their **name** (and mobile) and they are added.
3. Choose **Department** and **Service**. Fee and charge fill in. Fill the extra boxes (journey, devotees, etc.).
4. Enter **amount received** and how they paid. **Create booking**.
5. On the booking page: the **message** is ready in Telugu or English › **Open in WhatsApp** › send › **I sent it ✓**.
6. Need a receipt? Payments › **Receipt** › Print or Save as PDF.

## Moving work forward
- Open the booking › press the next **step** (Applied, Booked, Ready, Delivered …).
- Fill **Details** when done (PNR, ticket no, application no, dates, seat …) › **Save details**.
- The message for the new step is ready to send each time.
- Refund: Payments › choose **Refund given** › amount › Add. A refund receipt is made.

## Customers
- **Customers** › search by name, mobile or number. Profile shows family, consent, ID (last 4 digits only), documents and all their bookings.
- Always ask and tick **consent** for WhatsApp / SMS / calls.
- Upload Aadhaar / PAN copies only when needed, and set **Delete after**.

## End of the day
- **Accounts & cash** › check the **Day book** › count your cash › **Close my day**. The difference shows in red if it does not match.
- Used a portal wallet (IRCTC, AEPS…)? Record it in **Portal wallets**.

## Never
- Never share your password. Never send Aadhaar numbers on WhatsApp.
- Never book train tickets on a personal IRCTC ID – only the shop's authorised agent ID.
