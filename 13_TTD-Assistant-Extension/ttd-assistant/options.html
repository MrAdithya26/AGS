Security lock

Encrypts photo ID numbers on this computer (AES-GCM 256-bit, key from your master password). While locked, ID numbers are left blank when filling. The lock resets when the browser closes. If you forget the password, the ID numbers cannot be recovered; re-enter them.

Backup & restore

Save all profiles, passengers, photos, monthly dates and settings to a file, or move them to another computer. With the lock on, ID numbers stay encrypted in the backup.

How to use

  1. Under Profiles, create a profile (e.g. "My Family"), fill its address & contact, and add its passengers with photos if your seva form needs them. Add as many profiles as you like.
  2. Each month, open Monthly dates & reminders and enter the dates TTD announced. Reminders pop up 30, 15 and 5 minutes before.
  3. Before release time, log in at ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in, then pick the service (Special Entry Darshan, Arjitha Seva, Angapradakshinam, Homam…), date and slot yourself.
  4. When the pilgrim details form appears, the profile picker pops up on screen. Choose a profile, tick passengers and click Autofill. You can reopen it any time from the 🙏 Adithya TTD button or press Alt+Shift+F.
  5. If a box refuses autofill, orange Click & Paste buttons appear. Click one, press Ctrl+V, move on. Buttons turn green ✓ when correct.
  6. For photo uploads, click Attach photos, or drag a thumbnail from the panel onto the upload box.
  7. Check every field, type the CAPTCHA, and submit and pay on TTD yourself.
This extension never fills CAPTCHA, date, slot, amount or declarations. It never submits, pays or bypasses TTD's queue or protections. It is an independent helper, not affiliated with or endorsed by Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams. It sends no data anywhere.
