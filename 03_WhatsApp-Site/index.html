Questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a service?

Tap “Book” on any service and send the request on WhatsApp, or call us directly. We confirm the time and price before starting.

Do you provide doorstep service?

Yes. Home repairs, cleaning, delivery, events and many online services (document pickup) are available at your doorstep.

What documents do I need for online services?

Usually your Aadhaar, a mobile number linked to Aadhaar, and the documents for that service. Message us and we'll send you the exact list.

How are charges decided?

Government fees are charged as per official rates plus a small service charge. For repairs and events we give you a quote upfront, so there are no hidden costs.

What if my service isn't listed?

Just tell us. If you need something, we'll find a trusted person or shop to get it done.

Need something done today?

Message us now. We usually reply within minutes.
